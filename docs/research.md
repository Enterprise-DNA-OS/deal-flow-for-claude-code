# Why Affinity, checked 10 October 2026

Affinity is a relationship CRM for deal teams: venture and private equity funds, investment banks and advisers. Private capital deal flow was not yet a family in the rebuild registry.

**The bill.** Affinity's [pricing page](https://www.affinity.co/pricing) lists Essential at $2,000, Scale at $2,300 and Advanced at $2,700 per user per year, billed annually, with Enterprise on custom pricing. The page does not name the currency. Ten users on Advanced is $27,000 a year from the published rate. This is a published-rate calculation, not an invoice.
